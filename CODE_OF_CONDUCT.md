# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be decent to people. Harassment, insults, and dismissal are not welcome here, and neither is
  "just joking" about them.
- Criticise code, not people. Say what the problem is and what you would do instead.
- Respect that maintainers and contributors answer in their own time.
- Assume good faith, and ask a question before deciding someone was careless.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions, and any other space connected
to this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer (@erikenz on GitHub). Reports are read in
confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
